Be fully responsible for behavior during training period.

Follow Dress Code for Medical Students and Doctors in Training (HWP-HDO-IPP-012) if joining medical team.
لا يتحمل المستشفى أي مسؤولية عن الحوادث أو الأضرار خلال فترة التدريب.
SQUH is not liable for accidents or harm during training.
أي مخالفة قد تؤدي إلى إيقاف التدريب فورًا وإبلاغ الجهة الطالبة.
Any violation may result in immediate discontinuation and reporting to requesting institute.
إعادة بطاقة التدريب في اليوم الأخير؛ التأخير لأكثر من 5 أيام يؤدي إلى مصادرة الوديعة القابلة للاسترداد.
Return training ID card on the last day; delay more than 5 days forfeits refundable deposit.

Training card is property of SQUH and misuse/loss may lead to legal action.

Fees & Notes
  • Clinical Attachment Access / Observer ship: OR.23 (OR.20 refundable + OR.3 card cost).
  • Non-Clinical Attachment: OR.13 (OR.10 refundable + OR.3 card cost).
  • No facility/provision for transport, food, and accommodation.
  • Non-government doctors (clinical access/observer ship): OR.100 per month.
  • Non-government nurses/allied professionals (clinical access/observer ship): OR.75 per month.
  • Completed form should be submitted at least 4 weeks before proposed attachment date.
